A robot needs to maintain personal boundaries while staying efficient and avoiding unnecessary exploration. This is where Mission 1 comes in: BFS and A* both found a 13.5-meter path on the open map, but A* explored 700 cells instead of 928. The heuristic worked. In Mission 2, DFS planned a 50-meter route on the constrained map but ended with controller_failed, while A* completed its shorter route. Nav2 was the fastest successful planner on both maps, taking 35.40 seconds on the open map and 73.56 seconds on the constrained map. In Mission 3, A* social_2 kept 0.79 meters of clearance, while RRT social_1 kept 1.75 meters. Human-aware settings can create more space. Accounting for person or object buffers may improve safety, but it also increases speed. It also reduces where the robot searches, potentially shifting the exploration burden to future bots/routes.
